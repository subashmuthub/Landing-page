    Questions

    Who can take part?

    Any resident of Tamil Nadu aged 6 to 34 on 1 December 2026.

    What if my age fits two categories?

    Your category follows your age on 1 December 2026. The form suggests it when you enter your date of birth.

    What documents do I need?

    An age proof such as an Aadhaar card, school ID or birth certificate, shown at your district round.

    Is there a fee?

    No. Registration and participation are free.
